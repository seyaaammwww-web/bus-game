   - 3-note ascending melody (800→1000→1200 Hz)
   - Positive confirmation feedback
   - Integrated in: Game.tsx `handleSubmit()`

   - Single 600Hz tone
   - Subtle UI feedback
   - Integrated in: Game.tsx `handleBusComplete()`

   - 4-note ascending alarm (800→1100 Hz)
   - Creates excitement for special mode
   - Integrated in: Game.tsx `isRush effect`

   - Celebratory effect
   - Integrated in: Results.tsx with 1.5s delay
